03 · The deeper problem

Epistemic capture. Quietly, already, underway.

Three or four foreign commercial labs have become the cognitive default for a billion knowledge workers. Every prompt you type, every answer you accept without re-deriving, every framing you adopt because the model offered it first, sits inside a worldview shaped by their training data, their reinforcement choices, their system prompts, their hidden refusals.

You are not just renting compute. You are renting a frame on reality. And the frame has authors. You do not know them. You do not vote for them. You cannot inspect them. You cannot fork them.

Whoever owns the model that sits between you and the question owns the question.

The mechanism

Training data selection. RLHF objectives. System prompt overrides. Refusal patterns. Output throttling for certain queries. Search index weighting. The default-model setting. Each is a small editorial decision. Stacked, they constitute a worldview.

The compounding

Every quarter the share of human cognition mediated through these systems goes up. Every quarter the leverage of three or four labs over public reasoning compounds. Nobody voted for this. The Spalding Paradox: the entities with the largest commercial interest in the answer are increasingly the ones writing the question.

Not a conspiracy. A structure. 03 / 06
04 · The workforce reality

Knowledge workers are already on the leash.

The professional class has, in eighteen months, made AI assistance load-bearing for daily work. Juniors cannot draft a memo without Claude. Mid-level analysts cannot model without GPT. Senior partners review what their juniors generated with Grok. Engineers do not write code without Copilot. Marketers do not write copy without GPT. Designers do not iterate without Midjourney.

This was sold as a productivity tool. It has become an oxygen line.

The squeeze that is coming

The labs are losing money on every power user. They will price up. They will tier-gate the frontier model. They will rate-limit the heavy hitter. They will silently nerf the cheap tier. They will sunset the model your workflow depended on.

The slavery is structural

A knowledge worker who cannot afford the premium tier loses the ability to compete in a labour market where their peers can. A company that depends on a single AI provider to operate is one Terms-of-Service update from a continuity event. The means of cognitive production have been re-rented to a tiny number of landlords. This is not hypothetical. This is the third quarter of next year.

What it forecloses

Original thinking. Local language nuance. Heterodox positions. Anything outside the lab's training-time worldview. Slowly, then very quickly, public reasoning narrows to the band the labs sell.

Renting your mind has the same end as renting your home in a bubble 04 / 06
