05 · The commercial layer

Sovereign AI for executives and the companies they run.

For the executive

A personal operating system that runs on the operator's machine. Their notes, their strategy, their company's sensitive thinking, never leaves their device unless they choose. Voice in, voice out, mobile-first. The same demo you saw running my foundation today.

Pricing: paid tier for power users. Free tier for the baseline right to think.

For the company

A sovereign AI stack the company owns rather than rents. UK or Ireland hosted, open weights, auditable. No proprietary thinking shipped to a foreign vendor's training run. No vendor kill-switch on the day a model rev breaks the workflow.

Pricing: enterprise licence, support contract, optional 8GI-staffed deployment.

The right to communicate and the right to think have to be free. Complex services can be paid, and they should be. That is the cap table model: free for the baseline rights, paid for the operator-grade tools.

Why this is where the money lives

Executives already pay individually for ChatGPT Pro, Claude Pro, Grok Premium. Companies already pay for Copilot, OpenAI Enterprise, Anthropic Enterprise. We do not need to convince anyone the spend exists. We need to redirect it to a stack they actually own.
